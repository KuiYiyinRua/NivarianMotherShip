Bring the mothership to your colony. Expand Nivarian Race with 19 tactical supports, a customizable HUD, a points shop, buildable ships and an eight-stage Continuum campaign.

[h2]Your command center[/h2]

Check support costs, cooldowns and unlock requirements in one panel. Assign your favorite abilities to six HUD slots, from orbital fire and shields to fields, expedition legions and projected structures.

Bombard an area with solar fire or sweep the battlefield with a purification beam. Combine firepower, control and defensive support to suit the fight.

[h2]Warp Prism and energy field[/h2]

Switch the Warp Prism between flight and energy-field modes to establish a warp destination. Two-phase warping plays out at both the departure point and the destination.

[h2]The mothership points shop[/h2]

Search for goods, buy supplies with points or donate items. Trading requires a powered orbital trade beacon. Stock refreshes every seven days with up to 60 types of goods.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/15505053462094303961/D43CBB85D1626E09D3D427408CDC34F87AF7A1F3/[/img]

[h2]Research and the Continuum campaign[/h2]

Research support protocols at the uplink terminal. The eight-stage Continuum campaign is enabled by the military content built into Nivarian Race 1.7. On 1.6 it requires Nivarian Draconic Military; without that addon, support technologies can be researched directly. Later campaign missions include enemy ship reinforcements.

[h2]Optional research setting[/h2]

“Research technologies without completing their missions” is off by default. It removes only this expansion’s mission requirements for research. Prerequisite technologies, materials and normal research are still required. It does not complete technologies or missions, or bypass mothership support levels. Turning it off restores mission requirements and retains completed research.

[h2]Getting started[/h2]

Enable the dependencies below, then this expansion. Open the commander tab in the mothership support window and unlock support protocols at the uplink terminal.
